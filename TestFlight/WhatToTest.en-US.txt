Welcome to the first beta of Simple Inventory — a fast, tag-based stock tracker. Everything is new in this build; here's what to try.

Quick stock changes
• Tap + or − on an item to adjust stock by one; press and hold to count quickly
• Taps made while the screen is open group into a single history entry, with Undo
• Swipe right on a list item to add 1 without opening it; swipe left to remove 1 or delete
• Long-press a list item for +5 and +10 shortcuts and a quick preview
• Use Adjust for larger or backdated changes — type an amount or tap the presets

Items at a glance
• Every item shows a ring that fills against your low-stock limit, so you can see how close to empty it is without reading numbers
• Items turn orange when running low and red when critical
• The bar above the list shows Low and Critical counts — tap a chip to filter
• Search can be scoped to Low or Critical items

Tags
• Assign tags by tapping chips right in the item form; create a new tag by typing its name there
• Filter the list by tag from the chip bar or the filter menu
• Tap a tag on an item's screen to see everything else with that tag
• Rename, recolor, or delete tags from the filter menu's Edit Tags

Item details
• Stock history reads like a statement: each change shows the resulting total, grouped by day
• A trend chart shows stock over time against your warning levels
• Tap the Low and Critical chips to change warning levels in place
• Swipe a history entry to edit its date or delete it
• Add a photo with the camera or your library — tap the square next to the item name to change it

Things to test
• Sync between devices (data is stored in your iCloud)
• Deleting items, tags, and history entries — nothing should crash or leave a stale screen
• VoiceOver and larger text sizes
